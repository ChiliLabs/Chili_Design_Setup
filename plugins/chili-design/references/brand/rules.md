# Brand rules (Chili Labs' own materials)

Applies to decks, slides, posts and covers made with `chili-brand`. For this work these rules **replace** `ui-craft` step 0 and the «AI-generic» list in `../taste/core.md`. Client app UI keeps the UI rules.

Source of truth: Brandbook (Figma `YKyJFLqhZZJHHgTDTVadiO`, page Presentation) → Slides template (Figma Slides `A8CKFeaeoTaLaXmGGJaXnv`) → website DS (Figma `9oBkAkQMIrMz56atmaiiIE`). The old presentation DS (`8ESnHWxvuqWugmPmIbOpZS`) is **outdated** — don't use it. «Design [NEW]» page in the Design Concept file is a future direction — not used yet.

## Allowed in brand work (would be banned in UI)
- **All caps** — poster headlines (GT Walsheim Pro Condensed Black), social rubrics («MOBILE APP CASE», «ASK ME ANYTHING»), speaker names on covers. Not in body text, not in light-mode card titles.
- **Big number + small label** — stats and «about us» slides (10+, 100+, 5.0, 89%). Number is the point of the card; label 1–2 lines.
- **3D objects** — fire, chili, wrench, check badge, star, app icons, glass ribbon. Only from the designer's library or the source files; max 1–2 per slide, overlapping a card edge or corner.
- **Blue #5150EA as a full background** — the brand blue, not a «purple gradient».
- **Tilted elements** — highlight plates, price cards, photos, QR cards: rotate 2–6°, one or two per slide.
- **Coloured hero cards** — red, black, blue, lime cards next to light ones.

## Still banned (brand edition of «avoid the AI look»)
- Gradients other than brand gradients (Cyan / Lavender / Peach, 3D ribbon art); gradient text; glows; neon.
- Glassmorphism, blur cards, 1 px border + big soft shadow; strokes on cards (exception: blue swatch outline on blue background).
- Emoji as icons (emoji in social copy is fine, sparingly).
- Generic stock or AI-generated people; fake logos, fake reviews, fake numbers, lorem ipsum, «John Doe».
- Identical card grid repeated slide after slide; every slide centred; same layout on neighbouring slides (see `layouts.md`).
- Dot separators (`·`, `•`) joining text in one line — use separate layers or a line break.
- Pill badge above every headline; icon-in-a-square above every heading.
- Vague slogans («unlock your potential», «next-level solutions»). Use real Chili facts and client results.
- More than one decorative system per slide: either geometric shapes **or** hand-drawn lines **or** 3D, not all three.
- Fonts outside `tokens.md`; colours outside the palette.
- Test: if someone would believe «AI made this» at first glance, rework it.

## Checklist
- [ ] Mode chosen per slide; mix is intentional (rhythm, not random).
- [ ] Logo and link in the frame; caption with red dot on light content slides.
- [ ] Palette, fonts, radii from `tokens.md` only.
- [ ] Neighbouring slides differ in central layout; one hero element per slide.
- [ ] Highlight plate on max one word/phrase per headline.
- [ ] Every number and award has a source; nothing invented.
- [ ] Copy in English, short, in the brand voice (`voice.md`).
