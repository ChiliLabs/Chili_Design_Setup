# Social media

Source: Figma «Social Media — New Brand Book» (`vd2MpSoTEwPGbcVonXrlBk`, node 1319:5071) and brandbook slides Social media / Instagram / Event cover.

## Formats
- Post / carousel slide: **1080×1350 (4:5)**. Square 1080×1080 only if asked. Event cover: post 4:5 + wide banner (≈ 16:9).

## Anatomy
- Top: optional rubric in small caps (Medium) — «MOBILE APP CASE», «ASK ME ANYTHING:», «2025'S», «WE ARE LOOKING FOR» (sentence case allowed: «We Are Looking For»).
- Headline: GT Walsheim Pro Condensed Black caps, 2–4 lines; one key word/phrase in a contrasting colour (e.g. blue on pink, lime on blue, red on lime) **or** on a white/lime plate.
- Body: short GT Walsheim Regular text or white rounded cards with small text and a highlighted word.
- Bottom: optional pill CTA («HERE ARE 5 THINGS THAT MATTER RIGHT NOW»), black or white.
- Decoration: one hand-drawn line or blob cropped by the edge; photo cut-out of a person breaking into the line.
- Carousel counter top-right («2/5») in light text.

## Backgrounds
Blue, red, pink, lime, black — one per post. Carousel: alternate colours slide to slide, keep headline position.

## Post types
| Type | Layout |
|---|---|
| Case (carousel) | cover with phone mockup → «So, what's the result?» cards → «Our solution» list with small icons → mission |
| Hiring | «We Are Looking For» + role on a white plate, big loop line |
| Event recap | «HOW IT WAS? TEAM BUILDING» + group photo (full or in white card) |
| CEO / AMA | rubric + headline + cut-out portrait over a line/blob |
| Playbook / tips | dark or lime cover, line, pill CTA, then tip slides |
| Quote / question | scalloped frame, centred caps text, highlighted words |

## Event cover
Blue background, «Product Meetup #16» (number in lime), date, time, place, speakers' photos on soft wave, partner logos (Chili + venue) at the bottom, small yellow «spark» lines.
