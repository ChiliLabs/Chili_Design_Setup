# Logo, shapes, imagery

## Logo
- Script wordmark «Chili». Lock-up with tagline «WE MAKE MOBILE APPS» (Montserrat Bold, caps) only on logo / brand slides.
- Colour: **white** on blue, black, red and dark photos; **red `#FF3A46`** on white, light tints, lime, light blue, pink.
- On coloured plates: plate radius ≈ 24–32 (brandbook «Usage on backgrounds»).
- No official clear-space or minimum-size rule — keep at least the height of the «C» around it and never smaller than ~ 24 px high on a 1920 slide. Don't recolour, stretch, outline or add effects.
- Use the logo asset from the source files; never retype it in a font.

## Geometric shapes (brandbook)
Flower/blob, rounded cross, half-circle, step block, leaf, spring/coil, soft X. Solid brand colour (usually lime on blue). Use 1–3 per piece, large, cropped by the edge; never as icon substitutes.

## Hand-drawn lines (brandbook)
Thick single-stroke loops and squiggles with round caps: lime on blue, pink on blue/red, white on blue, black on lime. Cropped by the slide/post edge, running behind content. One line per slide (two on covers). Also: the wavy road path behind agenda numbers and speaker photos.

## Frames and plates
- Highlight plate: lime (or white/black) rounded rectangle behind one word of a caps headline, tilted 2–5°.
- Scalloped (cloud) frame: black or pink panel with a scalloped edge on a contrasting background — quotes, agenda, «question to the audience».
- Soft wave / blob under photos on event covers (light tint on blue).

## Photos
- Real Chili team, office, events, client app phones in hand — supplied by the designer. No stock people, no AI-generated faces.
- Speakers: square photos with radius 40 on a coloured tile (red, pink), name in caps + role below.
- Phones: real app screens from Chili cases; phone in hand on blue or full-bleed dark scene.

## 3D
- Library supplied by the designer: fire, chili, wrench, check badge, star, plus, palette, app icons, glass ribbon.
- 1–2 per slide, overlapping a card edge/corner or sitting at the bottom-right of a card. Not in body text, not as bullets.

## Icons
- Small line/flat icons inside chips (process steps). Source: the brandbook / designer's icon set — confirm the set before use (open question). No emoji as icons in decks.
