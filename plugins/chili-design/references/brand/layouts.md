# Central-block layouts

The frame is fixed; the **central block is where we experiment**. Our decks repeat a few families but vary the composition inside them — keep that balance: recognisable system, no two neighbouring slides alike.

## Families (bento grids in the content area)
| Code | Composition | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | one large panel (radius 60) with content inside | process, timeline, diagram |
| A2 | large photo/visual left ~40% + text column right | about, case intro, simple slide |
| A3 | text card left + large visual card right (60/40 or 50/50) | partner, feature, testimonial |
| B1 | 3 equal columns | 3 services, 3 stats, 3 steps |
| B2 | 4 cards: 1 tall + 3 (2+1) | about us, team, stats mix |
| B3 | tall card + 2×2 or 2×3 small grid | values, features list |
| B4 | 2 big cards + 1 small connector element (3D plus, arrow) | comparison, estimate, before/after |
| C1 | asymmetric stack: one wide card on top + 2–3 below | agenda, overview |
| C2 | staircase / diagonal flow of chips or cards | process, growth |
| C3 | overlapping elements: tilted cards, 3D objects over corners, photo breaking the card edge | pricing, hero slides |
| D1 | statement: one big number or quote, little else | key fact, section opener |
| D2 | full-bleed photo with text overlay | cover, case opener |

## Axes to vary (pick 2+ per slide vs. its neighbours)
- Split ratio: 50/50 · 60/40 · 33/33/33 · 1 tall + grid.
- Card count: 1 → 6.
- Hero colour: black · red · blue · lime · photo · none.
- Hero position: left · right · top · centre.
- Depth: flat · tilted card · 3D overlapping edge · photo breaking the frame.
- Content type: number · photo · phone mockup · list · quote · diagram.
- Density: airy (1–2 cards) ↔ dense (5–6 cards).

## Variety rules
- Neighbouring slides never share the same family code **and** hero position.
- In any 4 consecutive content slides use at least 3 families.
- A family may repeat in the deck (that's the system) — change at least two axes when it does.
- Every 4–6 slides add a rhythm break: poster divider, D1 statement or D2 photo.
- Max one «trick» per slide (tilt **or** overlap **or** big 3D).
- Content decides the layout: don't put unlike things in an identical grid; don't invent cards to fill a grid.

## Exploration step
For each content slide: sketch 2–3 options from different families (wireframe in chat or quick frames), state in one line why the chosen one fits the content, then build. When asked for «more creative», push the depth and asymmetry axes first (C3, B4, tilt, 3D overlap), keep the frame and tokens.
