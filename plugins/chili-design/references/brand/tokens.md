Light-mode card fill in the Slides template ≈ cool light grey-blue (close to `#F2F5FB`); prefer `#F2F2FF` / `#F9F9FB` unless the template component gives its own.

Pairings that work: lime on blue · black text on lime · pink on red · pink lines on blue · white on red/blue/black · red logo on any light or lime surface.
Text on lime, pink, light blue = black, never white. Red text only for one accent word on light backgrounds.

## Typography
| Use | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Everything by default | **GT Walsheim Pro** Regular / Medium / Bold | tracking −0.5…−2 on large sizes |
| Poster headlines, social headlines | **GT Walsheim Pro Condensed Black**, ALL CAPS | line height ≈ 1.0–1.2 |
| Big stats numbers (poster) | GT Walsheim Pro Condensed Bold | |
| `%` sign next to big numbers | **Barlow Condensed Bold** | intentional |
| Logo tagline «WE MAKE MOBILE APPS» | Montserrat Bold | only in the logo lock-up |

Slide scale at 1920×1080 (starting point, ≈ — check the template):
- Light cover title: 160–200, Regular/Medium, tight leading, ends with a red `.`
- Poster headline: 120–180 Condensed Black caps.
- Card title: 40–48 Medium · body: 26–32 Regular · small label: 20–22.
- Stat number: 120–160 (light) / 250–300 (poster) · label 22–28.
- Caption (bottom-left): 28 Regular + red period.

Website scale (web only): H1 76 · H2 61 · H3 49 · H4 39 · H5 31 · H6 28 · body 20 / 18 · numbers 120.

## Radii
- **60** — large blocks: hero cards, photo panels, cards taller than ~half the content area, big poster panels.
- **40** — regular bento cards and medium photos.
- ≈ 24 — small cards inside cards (chips with icons, QR cards, list rows).
- Pill (full) — tags («Most chosen», «Estimate»), buttons, highlight plate ≈ 24–32.
- Circles: round arrow button, avatars, number markers.
Website radii (web only): 80 / 56 / 32 desktop · 56 / 40 / 32 tablet · 32 / 32 / 24 mobile.

## Grid and spacing (slide 1920×1080, ≈)
- Side margins 60. Logo top-left ≈ 60 / 50; `chililabs.io ↗` top-right, small, secondary text colour.
- Content area ≈ y 160–920. Caption baseline ≈ y 1010, left 60.
- Gap between bento cards 24–32. Card inner padding 40–56 (large) / 32 (small).
Social post 1080×1350 (4:5): margins 56–64; headline block at top.
