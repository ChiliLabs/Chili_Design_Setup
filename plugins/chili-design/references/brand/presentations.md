# Presentations

Slide 1920×1080. Default for client decks: **light bento** (Slides template). Mix modes for rhythm — e.g. dark photo cover → light bento content → poster divider → light content → poster or dark closing slide.

## Fixed frame (every content slide)
- Logo «Chili» top-left; `chililabs.io ↗` top-right.
- Content area in the middle (see `tokens.md`).
- Light mode: caption bottom-left — slide name in sentence case ending with a **red period**: «About us.», «Our Awards.», «Trusted Flutter Partner.».
- Poster mode: logo top-right in white, no caption.

## Modes
**Light bento** (Slides template) — white slide, cards in light tint + 1–2 coloured hero cards (black, red, blue), 3D objects overlapping card edges, phones in hand on blue, photos with radius. Calm, premium.
**Dark photo** (old Design Concept / Design Presentation covers) — full-bleed dark photo (phone on a dark/teal scene), huge white GT Walsheim Regular title bottom-left, round white arrow button bottom-right. For covers and case openers.
**Poster** (webinar, brandbook) — blue `#5150EA` (or light blue / black) background, Condensed Black caps headline, one key word on a tilted lime plate, hand-drawn lime/pink lines, scalloped frame, coloured stat cards. For webinars, events, dividers, agendas.

## Deck structure (client deck, default)
Cover → About us → (Services / Process) → Proof (Awards, Team, Testimonials, Trusted partner) → Content for this client (concept, scope, estimate, plans) → After delivery → Contacts / end. Cut what the brief doesn't need.

## Rules
- One idea per slide; headline ≤ 8 words.
- Light mode: max 2 coloured hero cards per slide; the rest light.
- Prices, terms, estimates only from the designer/PM — never invent.
- Speaker notes or long text go to the designer, not on the slide.
