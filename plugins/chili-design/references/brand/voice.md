# Voice (English only)

Confident, warm, plain. Outcome first, then proof. No jargon, no hype words.

- Headlines: short and concrete — «We make extraordinary mobile apps», «From idea to launch — we make development stress-free», «Feeling stuck? Chili can help», «How to prevent bad UI/UX ruining your business».
- Light-mode captions: noun phrase + red period — «About us.», «Team.», «Client testimonials.».
- Bold the proof inside a sentence: «Recognized as **No.1 in Latvia** and **No.9 worldwide** by the Flutter community».
- CTAs: «Start a project», «Discuss your idea», «Schedule free consultation», «See case study», «Let's build products together!».
- Values words: Technical expertise · Clear communication · Predictable delivery · Long-term partnership (one per card — not joined with dots).

## Facts (verify before use)
From chililabs.io and the Slides template — always confirm with the designer, add `(check: <month year>)` in the report:
10+ years · 40+/47+ team members · 80+/100+ completed projects · 150 clients · 5.0 Clutch · Red Dot winner · Official Flutter Consultants (Google) · Top 10 Flutter app developers worldwide.
Different sources give different numbers — ask which is current. Never invent clients, quotes, prices or awards.
